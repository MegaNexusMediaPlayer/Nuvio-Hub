# Nuvio Hub 6.0.29 — glass look

Prepared 1 October 2026 from the 6.0.28 test build (themes; not released).

## Glass

* The grey boxes behind posters and catalog cards (visible wherever a card has
  no picture or a transparent logo, e.g. streaming-service logos) are now
  **glass**: a faint white layer, brighter at the top, with a light rim.
  Every card and poster has a **soft drop shadow** for depth.
* The **focus frame** is a glass ring with a soft glow (same light-blue tint).
* **HUB buttons** (skin Home and HUB Settings) and the MegaNexus header
  buttons are glass at rest and bright glass when focused.
* **Clock and weather** sit on a glass capsule.
* All of it works in the Light, Dark and Semi-dark themes.
* Kodi cannot blur what is behind a control; the frosted look comes from the
  translucent layer and rim.

## Poster and catalog transparency

* Posters and catalog art are drawn **10 % see-through** by default over the
  glass boxes. **HUB Settings → Home & appearance → Poster and catalog
  transparency**: Off / 10 % / 20 % / 30 %. Constant-alpha Kodi animations,
  no extra textures; cost is negligible.
